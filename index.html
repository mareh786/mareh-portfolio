01 About

I got into infrastructure the way most people do — by being the person who got paged when something fell over. Six years later I'm still the one who gets paged, but now the systems mostly fall over less: multi-tenant Kubernetes clusters, the observability stack that watches them, and the automation that keeps both from needing me at 2am.

I care most about the boring stuff done well — dashboards people actually read during an incident, alerts that mean something when they fire, and infrastructure changes that are reviewable instead of tribal knowledge. If a runbook is longer than a page, I'd rather fix the system than write a longer runbook.

  • Kubernetes reliabilitymulti-cluster, multi-tenant, at scale
  • Observability & dashboardingmetrics that people actually read
  • Infrastructure automationless manual toil, fewer 2am pages
